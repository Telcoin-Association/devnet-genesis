## Why

Issue #1850 (gap G1): when the WHOLE network leaves one epoch's record uncertified, every node's record collector stops at that epoch forever on main: later epochs are certified by their own vote rounds, but a node that was not in those rounds (an observer, a node that was down) can never collect them.
The stack fixes it behind three switches (`tasks/bench7/briefs/e3.md`, `e4.md`; names verbatim in `tasks/checkpoint-bench7-E3.md`, `-E4.md`):
- `epoch_record_collect_past_uncertified`: a collector walks past an epoch it holds as its OWN uncertified record (never for a node without that record; at most 16 gaps);
- `epoch_record_recertify`: the close-time failsafe also re-votes one older own uncertified record (16-epoch lookback), with verified signatures kept across rounds;
- `epoch_record_conflict_halt`: a validated conflicting certified record stops the node (not exercised here: no test may trigger it; assert its counter stays 0).
A test hook makes the uncertified epoch on demand (`tasks/bench7/briefs/e5a.md` and the unit's hand-back in `tasks/checkpoint-bench7-E5a.md`): `TN_TEST_WITHHOLD_EPOCH_VOTE=<epoch>` with `TN_TEST_WITHHOLD_EPOCH_VOTE_ROUNDS=<n>` on two validators of four leaves that epoch's record with 2 of the 3 votes it needs. **Use ROUNDS=2**: with the default 1, main's own failsafe certifies the record one close later (that claim comes from reading the code: your first run can confirm it cheaply by logging what happens; report it).

## The tests (new file `crates/e2e-tests/tests/it/epoch_record_walk.rs`, ONE `mod` line in `tests/it/main.rs`)

Four validators as processes, short epochs (take the epoch length and the helper calls from `epoch_close_forms.rs` on your base; the withheld epoch K = 1 so epoch 0 closes normally first), light load across the closes, validators A and B carry the hook for K with ROUNDS=2, C and D do not. One shared driver, three tests (the timelines of the hook unit's hand-back §5):
1. `test_uncertified_epoch_stops_the_collector_on_main` (all three record switches OFF: documents the gap): after the close of K+2 (the failsafe round of K+1 has given up by then) record K is uncertified on all four nodes; records K+1 and K+2 are certified on the four validators (their own rounds); an **observer** started AFTER the close of K+2 on a fresh datadir (or stopped before the close of K+1 and restarted after the close of K+2: choose what the helpers support, the design says `common.rs`'s observer start) holds no certified record K+1 or K+2 within 60 s; the gauge `tn_state_sync.epoch_records_certified_through` reads K−1 = 0 on every validator.
2. `test_uncertified_epoch_walked_past` (`epoch_record_collect_past_uncertified` ON on every node, recertify OFF): record K stays uncertified; the validators' collectors walk past K (line "epoch record collector walking past an uncertified epoch", `tn_state_sync.epoch_record_gap_walks_total` > 0 on at least the nodes the code makes walk: establish which from a first run and the code, then assert exactly that); an observer that holds its OWN record K (it was running at the close of K: stop it after the close of K and before the close of K+1, restart it after the close of K+2 with the walk on) serves certified records K+1 and K+2 and not K; an observer WITHOUT record K (fresh datadir after the close of K+2) still stops at K: assert it (this is the design's rule "never for a node without that record", and the thing a reviewer will ask). State what the gauge reads on each kind of node and assert it.
3. `test_uncertified_epoch_recertified` (`epoch_record_recertify` ON on every node; the walk ON too, as in the bench 7 arms): record K is certified on all four validators shortly after the close of K+2 (the pick at that close is K; line `epoch record re-certify round` with `epoch=K`; `tn_node.epoch_record_recertify_rounds_total{result="certified"}` > 0 on at least one node: say on which the code guarantees it); after that the gauge reads the last closed epoch on every validator, and an observer started fresh afterwards collects every record including K.
In every test: every node's `node switches` line carries the keys with the values the test set (a key the node does not know is ignored silently); no node logs any fatal line of the shared list (`FORK_LOGS` or its name on your base); `tn_state_sync.epoch_record_conflicts_total` is 0; each validator with the hook logs `test hook: withholding own epoch vote` with `epoch=K` and `round=1`, then `round=2`, and no node without the hook logs it; the chain keeps advancing through every close (block number and epoch on all four).
How a test reads a node's certified records: use what `epoch_close_forms.rs` already does on your base (its RPC or log helper for "node X holds certified record E"); if it is private there, make it `pub(crate)` in a visibility-only commit.
