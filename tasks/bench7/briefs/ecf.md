## Source (read-only)

- `test_held_member_two_closes_{always,adaptive}`: one member trails the others by more than the vote fix's floor (8 outputs) at the close of epoch 0, is released before the close of epoch 1; every node holds both certified records, the held member did not sign the first, and each record's `final_state` is the block in which the chain concluded the epoch (established from the chain: `getEpochInfo(E+1).blockHeight`, the registry's epoch turning in block N, the 32-byte closing seed in `extraData`, the closing consensus header's digest as `parentBeaconBlockRoot`).
- `test_missed_record_round_{always,adaptive}`: two of four members miss the vote round of epoch 1's record (the node's own hook `TN_TEST_EXIT_BEFORE_EPOCH_RECORD`), the round gives up without a quorum, the chain goes on, and the close of epoch 2 certifies that record.
Port the file's final state as the first commit (body names the three bench-6 commits), then the extensions as further commits.
