# A4 — epoch closes, epoch records, the kill's recovery and the fail-stop census (bench 7, one run)

Unit `A4` (analysis, opus, read-only on code and results). Read `tasks/bench7/briefs/a-common.md` first.
Checkpoint `tasks/checkpoint-bench7-<tag>-A4.md`; output `benchmark/private/bench7-<run>/A4/`; `<big>` = `benchmark/work/bench7-scratch/<tag>-A4/`; budget under 200k tokens.
One extraction pass per node over the whole log (`logs/<node>-unit-stdout.log.gz`), four nodes at a time; the prefiltered logs for the header and commit lines.
A7 reads two keys of your `closes.json` for its fleet checks (`closing_block_check`, `certified_through_at_end`): write them as soon as questions 3 and 4 are done.

## Questions (tick each; checkpoint after each)

1. Per epoch close (every close, idle and loaded): signers (min / median / max over the validators' `epoch record quorum` lines; `tn_node_epoch_record_signers` after the close), the missing signers (`tn_node_epoch_record_missing_signers_total{signer,round}`, `round="current"` apart from `round="recertify"`), ONE record hash (every hash any node logged for the epoch: vote, quorum, fetched or recovered record, and the `digest` of `epoch record published`), ONE closing block (`final_state_number` / `final_state_hash` on every node's `epoch record published`), seconds from the close to the first quorum line and to the seventh vote, the commit gap across the close, the next epoch's first headers and commits.
2. Per node and close: `epoch record published` `branch` (`built`, `stored_matched`, `stored_restored`); whether every node published a record for every closed epoch (`arms.json` `epochs_without_record`, `epoch_records_reading`). To be checked on the first run: whether the observer publishes its own record (if it never does, say so and leave it out of that line).
3. The closing-block check (no switch): per node, the increase of `tn_epoch_epoch_record_closing_block_check_total{result}` over the run; every result `ok`, none of `no_block`, `beacon_root`, `nonce_epoch`, `extra_data_length`, `extra_data_seed` (the plan's "no `mismatched` count" means this: there is no label value `mismatched`); the number of checks against the closes and restarts the node ran (the check runs on the `built`, `rederived`, `stored` and `entry` paths); `tn_epoch_epoch_close_identity_check_total{result="mismatch"}` 0; no line `epoch record final_state is not the block where the epoch-closing calls ran`. Key `closing_block_check` (per node, per result) and `close_identity_check`.
4. The certified-through gauge: `tn_state_sync_epoch_records_certified_through` on all eleven nodes at the export's end against the last closed epoch (the highest `epoch` of `epoch record published`), and after each close the time until each node's gauge reached that epoch (15-s step; the collector updates within about 5 s, `tasks/checkpoint-bench7-E2.md` notes). Key `certified_through_at_end` (per node: gauge, last closed epoch, equal yes/no).
5. Conflicts and the collector: no line starting `epoch record conflict halt:` and none starting `epoch record conflict:` on any node; increases of `tn_state_sync_epoch_record_conflicts_total`, `tn_state_sync_epoch_record_conflicts_below_halt_threshold_total`, `tn_node_epoch_record_round_conflicts_total{round}`, `tn_state_sync_epoch_record_walk_parent_mismatches_total` (all 0 expected); walks past an uncertified epoch (`epoch record collector walking past an uncertified epoch`, `tn_state_sync_epoch_record_gap_walks_total`); `got an invalid epoch record` with its `peer`.
6. Vote rounds: short rounds without a certificate (`failed to reach quorum on epoch close`; on this build it is logged only when no certificate is held) and with one (`epoch vote round ended without a local quorum; the record is already certified`, `tn_node_epoch_record_round_ended_certified_total`); re-certify picks (`epoch record re-certify round`, `tn_node_epoch_record_recertify_rounds_total{result}`, `epoch record certified from kept votes`, `re-arming epoch vote rounds to re-certify older epoch records`, `tn_node_epoch_record_retained_votes`); any `own epoch record differs from a certified record in a re-certify round` with its `stop` value; how each node first held each certified record (`e4_holders.py` paths Q, S, F0, F1, R) and `retrieved cert for epoch` with its `peer` and `signers`.
7. The kill: validator-05 from the kill to `CvvActive` (`tn_node_mode` through `node_mode.py`, ±15 s, and the mode lines of its log), its rejoin episodes and the `rejoin not ready` reasons over time (`timeline.py`), what it replayed or fetched for a close inside its outage (and the `branch` it published for it), the promotion; its own work after the promotion (`own_after.py`); its catch-up against the fleet (`prom_recovery.py`); whether it signed the next record; its generator's landings through the outage (`blaster_landing.py`). Bench 6 measured 21m40.7s (run 2) and 22m39.8s (run 3) kill to `CvvActive` (`tasks/bench6-report-r20261113-1351.md` §8.3); explain any difference only with this run's own lines.
8. Every node that left `CvvActive` outside validator-05's outage (`node_mode.py` on all nodes), with times and reason lines.
9. The observer's restart: time to its first `catching up consensus blocks` line at `catchup_distance` 1 and to per-output following (`obs_follow.py`).
10. The epoch loop around the kill and the restarts: `skipping already-forwarded consensus output` by `path`, `tn_node_leftover_outputs_skipped_total`, `tn_node_leftover_output_gaps_total` (0 on an honest run).
11. The census over every whole log: each fail-stop phrase of a-common §9 (0 expected; the re-certify conflict counts only with `stop=true`), `we have forked!`, panics, the tn-lmdb latch (`every later write and commit fails`, `the final sync failed`), `test hook:` and `ignoring TN_TEST_` (none on a benchmark image); agree with `arms.json` `fail_stops`, `fail_stop_nodes`, `test_hook_lines` or say where not; warn and error templates new against bench 6 run 2's census (`benchmark/private/bench6-r20261113-1351/A3b/census.json`) or above ten times its rate per minute, per node and blast.
12. The arm on every node: each process's `node switches` line (the seven keys at the arm's values, a-common §1) and `tn_node_switch{name}` on all eleven nodes; the storage engine on every node (`opening database` `backend=`, `arms.json` `store_by_node`).

## Do not conclude

- That the closing-block check "works" from all-`ok` counts: with no mismatch to catch, the run shows only that it never stopped an honest node.
- A close's margin (signers, seconds to quorum) from one close or one run; the form's effect on a close or on recovery (A9 compares; bench 6 found none).
- That a short-round line is a failure when the record was certified (bench 6 called the error line a false alarm; on this build the certified case logs the info line instead: count both).
