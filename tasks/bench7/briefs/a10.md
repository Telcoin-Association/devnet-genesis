## What changed between the benches (the frame for every attribution)

- The storage engine: MDBX (bench 6, with `mdbx_random_advice` on) to tn-lmdb (#1834).
- The order hold `digest_order_hold` on (#1807); bench 6 ran with `sealed_tx_keep` and `digest_dedupe` off, and both switches are gone.
- The base: bench 6's image 2df831e3c against upstream main `45dce8947`, which also brought the vote-handler rework (#1822/#1839/#1842/#1844/#1846) beside `vote_queue_fix`, the faucet guard, and, from the bench 7 topics, the epoch-record switches (#1850), the closing-block check (#1851) in place of `epoch_record_state_check`, the orphan re-pool counters and the epoch-loop fixes.
- The adaptive window counts headers built, not rounds (a-common §3).
- Unchanged by plan: the fleet shape, the disk, the zones, the generator 62bbd5f, the blasts and the chaos profile (question 1 checks it).
So more than "the storage engine and the hold" moved: the vote path moved too. Attribute a difference to one change only where a node counter isolates it, and say "not separable" where it does not.
