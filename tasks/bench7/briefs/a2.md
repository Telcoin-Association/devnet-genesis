## Questions (tick each; checkpoint after each)

1. Per validator and window (B0, B1, B2, B2-warmup, B2-first120, B2-prekill, B2-outage, B2-poststart, B2-postrejoin, B2's 300-s buckets, the three kill windows of a-common §4): own headers proposed (`header proposed`), certified (`quorum reached`), certified share, uncertified by `reason` (`header uncertified`), re-proposals (`re-proposing last header`); the fleet row; the lowest validator per window.
2. For A7, per validator and window, key `lost_own_headers` in `numbers.json`: own headers not certified, and of them those with `num_batches` > 0 (the ones that can leave a lane behind).
3. Votes as author (every voter's `vote path timing` with `author` = this validator, all ten logs): outcome counts (`created`, `behind`, `superseded`, `timeout`, `rejected`, …) and `behind` per proposed header. As voter (its own lines): outcome counts, `lock_ms` p50 / p95 / max, `prelock_wait_ms` p95, `exec_wait_ms` p95, `total_ms` p95.
4. The same from Prometheus per window (reset-aware increases): `tn_primary_votes_refused_execution_behind_total`, `tn_primary_vote_outcomes_total{outcome}` (compare with the log counts of question 3 and say where they differ), `tn_primary_vote_timeouts_total{stage}`, and from `tn_primary_vote_stage_seconds` the p95 of `stage="lock"` and `stage="prelock_wait"` (bucket increases).
5. Execution lag per node: `tn_primary_consensus_exec_lag_rounds` mean and max per window; `tn_primary_epoch_entry_rounds_behind` with `max_over_time` per window.
6. Round time per validator (ms per round from its own header rounds, median beside mean) and proposal-to-quorum delay p50 / p90 of its certified headers (same node, same clock).
7. Shares: own share of committed certificates (`certificate committed` origin, read on the validator's own log and on validator-01's), leader share from `scheduled leader` (scheduled, found, swapped), the far validators by measured round trip (`far_r3.py` on B0) and their committed share.
8. The vote form per validator: `exec_policy` and `exec_basis` counts, `exec_tip − exec_block` in blocks and in block-bearing outputs (p50 / p95 / max; `blockmap.pkl`), certified share by policy, and for `<form>` = `adaptive` the window entries (`tip` → `starved` transitions; the window counts headers built, a-common §3). Cross-check with `arms.json` `vote_forms_by_node` (`exec_policy_pct`, `exec_basis_pct`, `exec_lag_max`, `certified_by_policy_pct`) and give any difference.
9. Where the refusals fell: every `behind` answer joined to the author's header (policy, `exec_block`, `exec_tip`) and to the voter's own last executed tip (`deficit_blocks`), and every `superseded` answer joined to the header's policy and whether that header was certified anyway (`refused_r3.py`, `join_r3.py`, `matrix_r3.py`).

## Do not conclude

- Why a header was lost: the per-header join (which voters held the named block) is A3's, the hold's part A7's.
- A `superseded` answer is a recoverable refusal by design (`vote_queue_fix`), not a lost vote by itself; count what it cost only through the header's certification.
- When a vote reached its author is not logged: a round trip from `far_r3.py` includes the proposer's own work and is a ranking, not a network latency.
- No comparison with the other form or with bench 6.
