## Questions (tick each; checkpoint after each)

1. One row per own header of every author in B2-prekill, B2-poststart, B2 and B1, joined to every voter's answer (`perheader.py`); keep the pickles in `<big>/perheader-<window>.pkl` (A8 reads them). Say how many headers per window had no join and why.
2. Per author and window: headers, certified share, basis (`exec_basis` `tip` / `quorum` / `floor`) and policy (`exec_policy`), the distance named (`exec_tip − exec_block`) in block-bearing outputs (mean, p50, p95) and in seconds (name the conversion you use and its source), certified share by basis (`q2.py`).
3. Holders: how many of the nine other voters held the named block (their last logged `exec_tip` at or above it) at the proposal and at the author's next header; certified share by the number of voters that created a vote before the author's next header (`q2d.py`); the lost headers that six or more other voters had executed, and what those voters answered (`q2b.py`).
4. Under-held headers: per header, held by at most five other voters at the proposal; per round, the count of such headers; the share of rounds with four or more (A8 reads this from your pickles; give the fleet figure here) and the certified share of those headers.
5. For `<form>` = `adaptive`: per author the starved windows (`q5_windows.py`): entries per hour, episode length in headers, rounds and seconds, the share of the author's headers inside a window, certified share inside and outside, headers lost per return to the own tip; how many proposers sat inside a window at once and what the rule named at each count (`q6_concurrency.py`). State in the table caption: the window counts HEADERS BUILT on this build (a-common §3), so the share is not comparable with bench 6 run 3's (`benchmark/private/bench6-r20261113-2118/I-10/q5-*.json`) without that note; the window caps at 128 and resets after a streak of 64 (contract §2.6). For `always`: write the window tables as n/a with one line why.
6. For `<form>` = `always`: the share of headers whose quorum value sat above the floor (basis `quorum` against `floor`) and the share capped at the own tip (basis `tip`), per author.
7. B2-first120 per header (bench 6 joined no header in that window): certified share by policy and basis, and the distance named.
8. Arithmetic, labelled as such: what floors 8, 16 and 32 would have named on this run's measured tips and how many voters held that block (`q4.py`); the tips and rounds are held fixed, so this is not a result.
