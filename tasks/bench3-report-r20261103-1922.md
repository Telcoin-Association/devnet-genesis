# Bench 3 report: r20261103-1922 (adiri:v0.2.0-devnet on v2 nodes, Closed admission)

Private findings report for review. Run 2026-11-03 19:22Z to 2026-11-04 00:50Z. Evidence lives under
`benchmark/private/bench3-r20261103-1922/<unit>/` (A1 KPIs, A2 certification and vote path, A3 chaos and
boundaries, A4o operations, A4n node-side census, P provenance) and `benchmark/results/r20261103-1922/`.
Code is cited as `path:line @3f9b0709`; every cited line was checked with `git show 3f9b0709:<path>`.

## 1. Summary

Ten validators in ten GCP regions plus one observer ran adiri:v0.2.0-devnet (telcoin-network 3f9b0709) on v2 node
scripts with Closed admission, from one coordinated start (22:26:08Z, 11/11 in the same second), through a 40-min
mixed blast with the standard chaos plan. The chain never stalled, all seven epochs closed on time, and no node
panicked or logged an MDBX error. Throughput fell: 4,037 tx/s with all generators running against 5,434 in
r20261007-0316 (-25.7 %) and 3,890 in r20261006-0458; 12.21 M landed (-15.2 %), 13.47 blocks/s (-28.5 %), land p50
3.49 s (+43 %) (A1/kpis_table.md). The three findings that matter most:
1. Execution is the throughput ceiling. The engine was busy about 100 % of the load; each output's MDBX persist grew
   from 0.97 to 1.60 s p50 and wrote about 1.07 GB on a disk capped at 800 MB/s, and voters wait for it (A4n).
2. Strict multistream-select V1 makes every vote cost 2 x RTT (89 of 89 pairs). The idle round is 451 ms, not 350,
   and London and the Netherlands certify 5 % of their own headers when rounds are fast (A2).
3. The validator killed under load needed 36 min 10 s and an epoch boundary to rejoin, after 11 failed promotions;
   it made no own certificate in epochs 5 and 6 and refused 19,214 vote requests (A3, A4n).
Verdict: v0.2.0-devnet is stable but is not a performance baseline. The next bench needs the V1Lazy cherry-pick and
a rejoin fix; per-output persistence is the throughput ceiling (0316 already ran its engine 94 % busy).

### F1. Execution is saturated, and the per-output persist is the part that grows

Evidence (A4n/bottlenecks.md #1, A4n/census.md (c) and T8-T9; A1/kpis.md "Resources"):
- Engine busy fraction (committed sub-DAGs/s x mean execution s/output, nine healthy validators) was 0.44 in the
  preamble, then 1.00, 1.00, 1.02, 1.00, 1.09, 0.99, 1.02 and 1.04 in the eight 5-min buckets of mixed, and 0.15 in
  the cooldown. `tn_engine_queued_outputs` reached its cap of 8 on all 11 nodes, the observer included.
- Consensus commit to "executed" was -0.04 s idle and 6-12 s p50, about 20 s p95, 22 s max on healthy validators in
  mixed (v06: 22.5 s p50, 40.9 s p95).
- Per output, in-memory block execution took 0.46-0.65 s p50 (21-24 blocks, 29 ms a block against 3 ms idle). The
  gap before the next output starts, which is the persist, grew from 0.97 s p50 (23:35-23:45) to 1.60 s p50,
  2.4 s p95 and 5.7 s max (00:00-00:13); v01 and v04 agree (`A4n/scripts/persist_gap.py`). The persist is 60-72 % of
  the engine's busy time.
- Disk: 480 MB/s mean writes in mixed, all from the node process (`write_bytes`), while the MDBX file stayed at
  4.30 GB, so the writes are page rewrites. 480 MB/s / 0.45 outputs/s is about 1.07 GB per output commit; at about
  800 MB/s that is about 1.3 s, the size of the persist gap. Per-second peaks reached 860-880 MB/s in every 5-min
  bucket from 23:38, against 800 MB/s provisioned, with about 2,150 of 20,000 IOPS used: the disk is
  throughput-bound. PSI io rose from 17 % to 29 %. CPU averaged 20 % and PSI cpu stayed at or below 2.7 %.
- Cost per landed transaction doubled during the load: about 160 us at 23:33 (6.2 k tx/s) and about 310 us at
  00:03 (3.2 k tx/s); written bytes per landed tx went from about 80 KB to about 170 KB. That matches the 5-min
  throughput series (5,986 -> 3,045 tx/s) bucket by bucket.
- Against 0316 (same disk): the process write rate is 439 MiB/s median here and 157.6 there (2.8x) at 26 % lower
  throughput, while data-dir growth per landed tx is unchanged (reth 256 B/tx). 0316 wrote about 33 KB per tx at a
  busy fraction of 0.94. A4n reads the workload as different (contract calls); A1 found the txgen tree, mix and args
  identical. The 3.6x bytes per tx is therefore a lead, not a proven regression.

Mechanism (checked @3f9b0709):
- One output executes at a time: `pending_task: Option<PendingExecutionTask>` at `crates/engine/src/lib.rs:68`,
  `spawn_execution_task` at `:145`, intake gated on `self.queued.len() < MAX_QUEUED_OUTPUTS` at `:271`, with
  `MAX_QUEUED_OUTPUTS = 8` at `:52`.
- After the blocks execute, `persist_output_with_retry(&reth_env, &executed_blocks)` (`crates/engine/src/payload_builder.rs:302`)
  runs `persist_executed_output` (`crates/tn-reth/src/env/execution.rs:316`): `save_blocks(…, SaveBlocksMode::Full)`
  at `:331` and one `provider_rw.commit()` at `:344`, synchronously in the same blocking task. Output N+1 cannot start
  until N's commit returns.
- The engine reports the executed block afterwards (`spawn_engine_update_task`, `crates/node/src/manager/node.rs:1882-1895`,
  `recent_blocks().send_modify`). A voter waits for the proposer's execution block before it handles a vote
  request (`wait_for_execution`, `crates/consensus/primary/src/network/handler.rs:913`, then `Processing vote request`
  at `:922`), and consensus waits for the leader's execution block before emitting each committed sub-DAG
  (`crates/consensus/primary/src/consensus/state.rs:607-613`). The share of vote requests a node processed inside the
  persist window of the output holding the header's execution block was 99.1 % on v06, 55.2 % on v03 and
  5.5-22.3 % elsewhere (A4n/census.md T9).

Impact: the engine sets the chain's rate under this load and its cost per transaction grows as the persist grows,
which is the decline A1 sees. Every landed transaction carries the 6-12 s commit-to-executed delay. The same lag
gates votes (F4), lengthens the loaded epoch boundaries (F6) and sets the follower's catch-up rate (F8). Not
proven: whether execution or the vote path (F2) drives the halving of the loaded round rate (2.05 -> 1.03 rounds/s
during the load, A4n); the bucket-by-bucket match suggests coupling (A4n open question 1).

Protocol improvement: take the persist off the execution path, and stop voting from waiting on disk.

Patch proposal:
1. Background persistence. Return the executed blocks to the engine before `payload_builder.rs:302` and hand them
   to a persistence task that commits output N while N+1 executes against an in-memory overlay of the unpersisted
   blocks (the reth engine-tree persistence model). Bound the overlay to 1-2 outputs; block intake when it is full.
   Crash recovery re-executes from the last persisted output, which the consensus DB still holds.
2. Report the executed block to `recent_blocks` when execution finishes, not when the commit does, so
   `wait_for_execution` (`handler.rs:913`) and `state.rs:607-613` see executed-but-unpersisted blocks. Execution is
   deterministic, so a crash loses only work that will be redone with the same result; this needs a review of every
   reader of `recent_blocks`.
3. Find what the 1.07 GB per commit is: per-table page writes (`mdbx_stat` or reth db stats before and after a
   loaded window). Then batch several outputs per MDBX commit, and check which tables `SaveBlocksMode::Full` writes
   that a TN node does not read.
4. Stop-gap A/B to test the hypothesis before any code: the same fleet with 1,600 MiB/s provisioned throughput.

Test plan: an engine unit test that output N+1 starts executing before N's commit returns and that the persisted
head trails by at most the bound; a crash test that kills between execution and commit and checks the restarted
node reaches the same block hash; a Criterion bench of `persist_executed_output` on a 21-24-block output of the

### F3. The restarted validator loops between promotion and demotion

Evidence (A3/recovery.md (c), A3/recovery.json, A4n/bottlenecks.md #2):
- Validator-05 restarted at 23:50:15.55Z, went CvvInactive at 23:50:32 ("we are behind", exec_epoch 4), and followed

  each demoted 8-10 s later. It rejoined at the epoch-7 entry, 00:26:24.96, and assembled its first own certificate
  at 00:26:26.06. Restart to rejoin 2,170 s; demotion to rejoin 2,153 s; kill to first own certificate 2,471 s
  (0316: 1,451 s restart to rejoin, no loop).
- In every promotion window `exec_round` stood still. The node restored an empty DAG ("Recreating dag from last GC
  round: 416", "Dag is restored and contains 0 certs for 0 rounds" at 00:14:01.97), incoming certificates waited for
  missing parents, and its proposer re-sent a stale header that every peer refused: the same round-545 header from
  00:15:16 to 00:22:42 against peer limits of 623 to 1,440, then round 1,495; 1,203 `too old for max round` errors.
- After each demotion the follower made no progress for about 61 s until `catch-up stalled after retries, requesting
  the missing consensus range from the fetch task` (12 times), then promoted about 4 s later: a 75-s cycle.
- Side effects: v05 refused 19,214 vote requests as `too new for max round` (its committed round reads 0 while
  inactive, so max round = 0 + 50), which alone fails the live post-check "too new = 0"; it dropped 5,552 TooNew and
  15,525 `CertificateManagerOneshot` gossip certificates; it re-sealed the same 7,354-tx pool on every promotion,
  and the pool landed only in epoch 7. Two `FailedToReport` seal failures fell at demotions.
- The automated recovery block (`benchmark/report/kpis.py`) reads the first transient promotion as the rejoin
  (00:11:01, 1,215 s, 0 failed reports). Prometheus ends at 00:23:10, before the real rejoin. Use recovery.json.

Mechanism (checked @3f9b0709):
- Promotion: `rejoin_ready` (`crates/consensus/executor/src/subscriber.rs:71-82`) promotes when the handled output is
  within `rejoin_round_window` (20 rounds at gc_depth 50, `crates/types/src/primary/mod.rs:84-86`) of the newest known
  header, the header is fresh and the epoch is open; `catch_up_rejoin_consensus` then sends `NodeMode::CvvActive`
  and restarts the epoch tasks (`subscriber.rs:275-321`).
- Demotion: `behind_consensus` (`crates/consensus/primary/src/network/handler.rs:211-249`) demotes when
  `effective_exec_round + gc_activity_window < round`, the window being gc_depth - 10 = 40 rounds
  (`types/src/primary/mod.rs:72-74`). Promotion at 20 behind leaves 20 rounds, about 10 s at the post-load rate.
- On promotion the follower stops feeding outputs, and the active path has nothing to execute: the DAG rebuilt from
  the certificate store holds no certificates (`construct_dag_from_cert_store`, `consensus/state.rs:143-165`), so no
  local commit happens and `exec_round` cannot advance until missing parents arrive.
- The proposer seeds its round from the stored last header when it is in the current epoch and ahead of
  `primary_round` (`proposer.rs:212-227`) and re-proposes a stored header of the current round (`proposer.rs:812-818`)
  or after the max delay (`:1062`). `primary_round` was not advanced while following, so the stale round-545 header
  passed that filter on each promotion and peers refused it as too old.
- The vote-side refusal is `max_round = committed_round + gc_depth` (`handler.rs:847-858`); an inactive node's
  committed round does not move.
- The 61-s stall ends at `crates/state-sync/src/lib.rs:376`; A3 matched its length to `STREAM_RETRY_MAX_DELAY` = 60 s
  (`lib.rs:35`) but no retry line is logged at info, so that link is inferred.

Impact: a validator killed under load is out of the committee for the rest of the epoch, and with longer epochs or
continuous load it would cycle until the load ends. The committee ran on nine voters for 41 min, and from 23:45Z on
eight (F4). The node's own users are held: blaster-05 landed nothing from 23:46Z to the end of its run.

Protocol improvement: promote only when the node can make progress as an active member, and never propose from a
round the committee has garbage-collected.

Patch proposal (rule changes, all at the sites above):
1. Promotion precondition (`rejoin_ready`, `subscriber.rs:71`): add "the certificate store holds a quorum of
   certificates for a round within `rejoin_round_window` of the newest known header", so the DAG rebuilt by
   `state.rs:143-165` is non-empty. If the follower path does not write the certificates of the outputs it handles
   to the store, write them there while following.
2. Seed the proposer on promotion: set `primary_round` from the newest known consensus header's round before the
   epoch tasks restart, so `proposer.rs:212-227` drops a stored header older than that round, and never re-propose
   a header whose round is below `newest_round - gc_depth` (peers refuse it as too old).
3. Demotion grace (`behind_consensus`, `handler.rs:211-249`): for one `gc_activity_window` of committee rounds after
   a promotion, demote only if `effective_exec_round` has not advanced since the promotion, not on the round gap
   alone.
4. Back-off: after two failed promotions in one epoch, stay CvvInactive (following, not voting) until the next
   epoch boundary, and log it at warn. This bounds the churn even if 1-3 miss a case.
5. While inactive, answer vote requests with a distinct "inactive" error instead of TooNew, and let proposers skip
   the node for the rest of the round (the refusals are correct, the noise is not).
6. Harness: `benchmark/report/kpis.py` must take the last demotion-free promotion (or the first own certificate) as
   the rejoin, and Prometheus collection must run past the epoch after the chaos.

Test plan: `crates/e2e-tests/tests/it/restarts.rs` already has `test_restarts_lagged_under_load` (`:1828`, rejoin under
load within `LOADED_REJOIN_TIMEOUT` = 120 s). Extend it to assert no "we are behind" within 60 s after "attempting to

window estimate.

2. Bound the voter-side evaluation to about 2 x max_header_delay instead of `vote_timeout` (`handler.rs:705`), or

5. Add counters `votes_requested` and `votes_created` per node, and a debug line with the elapsed time when

### F7. The idle round is 451 ms, set by the 7th certificate; a fleet-wide round floor is the parameter lever

configured peers.

Impact: on the throughput side, small (a refused handshake every 20-30 s per network per validator). On
availability: a v01 outage would blind the observer. The admission gate's "denied > 0" is the normal state for this
topology.

Patch proposal (harness): list the observer in `trusted_nodes` on every validator, or, if single-homing is the
intended design (it is the devnet's hub rule too), make the observer dial only the peers in its own admission view
and teach the gate to expect it.

Test plan: next run, `denied_in` = 0 at step 10 and more than one consensus-output sync peer on the observer.
