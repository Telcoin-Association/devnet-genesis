## What #1870 says, and what it could not show

In bench 7, validators 02 and 06 ("far": their certificates usually form among the last of the ten) had their batch-carrying headers certified, yet many of those certificates were never committed; their batches came back only through the commit-path re-queue, tens of rounds later; transactions sent to them landed after 13 to 61 s against about 1 s on the others. The step it INFERRED: the near validators' round r+1 headers do not name the far validator's round-r certificate as a parent, because it arrives after they have built their parent set (in code: a late parent of an older round is dropped), so the far certificate is in nobody's causal history and no leader commits it. Bench 7 did not log parents.
