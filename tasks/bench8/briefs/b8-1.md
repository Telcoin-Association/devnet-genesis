## Part 2 — fields on `header proposed` (`proposer.rs` near `:526-545`)

Append, after the existing fields:
- `exec_quorum`: the block number of the quorum value BEFORE the floor and the bounds were applied (what a quorum of the parents' authors held), and `exec_floor`: the block number of the floor entry used. With `exec_block`, `exec_tip` and `exec_basis` already there, each header then shows how far the floor moved the choice. When the rule is off (`exec_policy=off` or `tip`), log 0 for both.
- `parents`: the authors of the parent certificates this header names, in a fixed order, compact (see the common brief). `num_parents` stays.
- `pending_unheld`: of the digests pending when the header was triggered (`pending_before`), how many were NOT held back by the own-chain order hold, i.e. could have gone into this header. (Bench 7's review of the hold proposed this field: held digests count toward the early-header trigger, so a trigger can fire on a backlog the header cannot carry.) Read `trigger.hold` and the hold's bookkeeping to derive it without a second pass over the queue; if it costs a pass over the pending queue per header, say so and bound it.
