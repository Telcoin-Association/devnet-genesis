## Why

#1868 lets a proposer name an execution block that a quorum of its parents' authors already held, instead of its own tip, so that voters do not have to wait for execution. It shipped two forms behind two switches: `quorum_exec_block` on with `quorum_exec_block_starved_rounds` 0 ("always-on"), and with a count above 0 ("adaptive": the rule applies only in a window opened by a cancelled header and closed by a streak of certified tip headers). The owner chose always-on as the only form that ships (bench 7: one run each; the adaptive form did not do better, and it carries a window, a streak and two constants nobody could tune). After you, every header's execution block is chosen by `choose_exec_block` with the floor of 8, always.
