# B8-4 — when a certificate becomes a parent candidate at each node

Common brief: `tasks/bench8/briefs/b8-common.md`. Skills: `tn-domain-consensus`. Worktree `/home/drtnarg/coding/telcoin/tn-b8-bench` (after B8-3). Checkpoint `tasks/checkpoint-bench8-B8-4.md`.

Bench 7's report and the issue telcoin-network #1870 infer that a far validator's certificate of round r is often not named by the near validators' round r+1 headers because it reaches them after they took their parents. Unit B8-1 logs which parents a header named. This unit logs when each certificate became available as a parent at each node, so the two can be joined.

Read `crates/consensus/primary/src/aggregators/certificates.rs` (`append_certificate` near `:24`, the send to the proposer `parents().send((parents, round))` near `:40`, `append` near `:75`) and its caller(s): how a certificate of round r reaches the aggregator, when the parents for round r+1 are sent to the proposer (at the quorum? again for each later certificate of the round?), and what happens to a certificate of round r that arrives after the proposer has built its round r+1 header.

Add one line per certificate that enters the round's aggregator at this node (own and peers'; about ten per round per node), `certificate accepted`, target `consensus::metrics`, with: `epoch`, `round`, `origin` (the certificate's author, in the short form), `round_certs` (how many certificates of that round this node has now, this one included), `parents_sent` (whether this node had already sent a parent set of that round to its proposer BEFORE this certificate), `late_ms` (0, or the milliseconds since that first send), and `source` if the caller knows whether the certificate was built here, received in a broadcast or fetched.
If a later certificate of the round makes the aggregator send an updated parent set, say so and add `parents_resent = true` on that certificate's line.

Tests: a unit test on the aggregator: ten certificates of a round in a fixed order; the lines' `round_certs`, `parents_sent` and the certificate at which the first send happens are as expected.

Hand back as the common brief says, plus two sentences, with file:line, on what the proposer does with a round r certificate that arrives after its round r+1 header was built (that is the step #1870 infers).
