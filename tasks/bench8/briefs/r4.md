## Why

#1869 adds the own-chain order hold: a digest of a worker lane is not put into a new header while an older digest of the same lane sits in an own header that is neither committed nor given up, so a cancelled or uncommitted header cannot have its digests re-queued behind newer ones (#1807). It shipped behind `digest_order_hold`, default off. After you the hold is the proposer's only behaviour.
