## What is known (the first look of run 2, one run, not yet figure-checked)

Run `r20261116-2332` (2026-11-17 UTC): ten validators and one observer under bench 7's load; epoch 3 from 00:43:43, epoch 4 from 01:03:52, epoch 5 from 01:23:52; the load blast's mixed phase 00:54:44 to 01:34:53; validator-05 killed on purpose at 01:22:43.
- Validator-10 ran 25 to 53 block-bearing outputs behind the fleet's leading execution tip in epoch 3 (from about 00:55) and answered `created` to 1,060 of about 20,800 vote requests in 00:54:44-01:08:21 (superseded 11,137, behind 8,038, rejected 560).
- At 01:03:43 its round-3276 header (epoch 3) drew `irrecoverable error requesting vote … Failed to return pending certificate manager result` from all nine voters (216 error lines).
- It then proposed no header for 541 s (01:03:43.2 to 01:12:44.7); in that gap it answered `behind` 15,821 times and `created` never; its log has 144 `Failed to track spawned task process-gossip…` and 44 `error while sealing batch` errors between 01:03 and 01:12.
- It proposed again from 01:12:44.7 and lost almost nothing afterwards.
