## Why

The stack #1862-#1869 shipped its fixes behind runtime switches that default to off. The owner's review decided that a fix the benchmarks confirmed is the node's behaviour, so the switches go. You remove two of the six:

- `epoch_record_collect_past_uncertified` (from #1866): the record collector walks past an epoch this node holds only as its own uncertified record.
- `epoch_record_recertify` (from #1866): vote rounds keep verified signatures across rounds and a close re-votes one older uncertified record of this node's own.

After you, both happen always. The stop on a certified record that conflicts with this node's own (unconditional on `main` since #1849) is not yours and does not change.
