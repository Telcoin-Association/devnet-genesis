# Runbook: devnet launch 2, 2026-11-17 (skeleton, filled in as run)

Launch 2 replaces chain 32285 with a fresh genesis in the shape mainnet will have: four closed
validators, a worker gateway in front of each validator's RPC, node records advertising the
gateway, a public RPC node (rpc-6) as the open hub, the committee P2P allowlist on each validator,
and node 5 staking after genesis. Design: `docs/mainnet-simulation-plan.md` (dg-l2). Plan:
`tasks/l2/plan-approved-2026-11-17.md` (Part C, W3). Interface: `tasks/l2-contract.md` (§8-§11, §14).
Where the plan and the contract differ, the contract and the driver headers are right.

  on `pgrep -f`. Beside it runs the token watcher of §1.1.
