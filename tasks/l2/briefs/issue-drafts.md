## Each draft: `tasks/issue-bodies/l2-<slug>.md`
- Line 1: `# <title>`: the title from the report's table (a full sentence stating the problem, at most 120
  characters; you may improve it, keeping the limit and stating a fact, not a wish).
- A two-line lead: what is wrong and who meets it.
- `## What happens`: the observed behaviour with the concrete numbers and the exact log or output lines.
- `## Evidence`: where each number comes from (run date and UTC time, host role, file under devnet-genesis
  `tasks/…`, command). Say "read from code, not run" or "not verified" where that is the case.
- `## Where in the code`: file and line at the commit you checked (give the commit), a few words on what the
  lines do. Verify every file:line a row gives against the source before you repeat it; if it does not match,
  give what you found. Leave the section out when the cause is not located, and say so under "What is not known".
- `## What is not known`.
- `## What could be done (suggestions, none tried)`: unless a fix exists (for example in PR #68: then name it).
- `## Related`: other drafts by slug in double braces, for example `{{joiner-sync-stalls-behind-allowlist}}`,
  PR #68 where it touches the same code, the plan's decision if one applies.
Length: 30-80 lines. One problem per draft; if the rows hold two separable problems, say so in the lead (as the
format file does) rather than writing two drafts.

## Beside each draft: `tasks/issue-bodies/notes/l2-<slug>.sources.md`
A table: every number, quoted line and file:line in the draft, with its source (challenge row, evidence file
and the line you grepped, or the `git show` command) and one of `checked` / `taken from the row, source not
re-read` / `not verifiable here`.

## Writing rules
- Plain, direct sentences: the fact, then the consequence. Short paragraphs or lists. No praise words, no
  "robust / seamless / comprehensive / crucial / critical", no rhetorical questions, no bold for emphasis
  inside sentences, no dash chains.
- Write for a maintainer of the TARGET repository: say "a devnet in mainnet shape (four validators behind
  worker gateways, a committee P2P allowlist, one public RPC node, a fifth node staking after genesis)" once
  instead of devnet-genesis jargon; do not use unit names (D3a, RH5, VA) or "the rehearsal's step 14" without
  saying what it is; host names like `validator-1-devnet` are fine with their role.
- Keep every qualifier of the source ("not verified", "inferred", "seen once").
- Refer to people by role, never by name. No private data: no email address, no secret names beyond what the
  scripts print, no internal IP addresses unless the problem is about them (public addresses of the devnet are
  fine to leave out too: prefer roles).
- No attribution lines, no "generated by" footer.
