| S3 | SHOULD | devnet-network-config.sh:292 `nc_field` (all NETCFG_FAST parsing: `nc_node_fast`, `nc_fast_check`, `nc_recheck`) | `nc_field` returns the FIRST `KEY=` line. `_tn_iap_retry` streams every attempt's stdout into the same capture (design D3 "verified facts"). After a transport fault in the middle of a read or write session, the failed attempt's partial `NC_B64=` line wins, and it may have the retry's first line glued onto it. The decode or `cmp` then fails, and a node whose install succeeded gets a spurious ❌ and the bench step dies. Test: a fake session prints half its output, then rc 255; the retry is ok (TN_IAP_RETRIES=1); the node passes with one write. |
