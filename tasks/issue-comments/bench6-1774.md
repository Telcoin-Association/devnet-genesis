Bench 6 data for this issue: the fix's refusal `batch … was already rejected in this epoch` appeared 177 times in [r20261113-1351](https://benchmark.telcoin.network/runs/r20261113-1351/) and 742 times in [r20261113-1630](https://benchmark.telcoin.network/runs/r20261113-1630/), at loaded epoch closes with every pool switch off, beside 575 and 1,427 `Invalid epoch` refusals in the same windows, as the batch builders retried across the boundary.
On main at 45dce8947 this is the refusal the fix intended: an `InvalidEpoch` or `InvalidBaseFee` result cannot change within a worker handler's epoch (`crates/consensus/worker/src/network/handler.rs:107-121`, `:200-207`; the validator takes the epoch and base fee at epoch start, `crates/node/src/manager/node/start_epoch.rs:926-927`), so refusing the repeat (`handler.rs:542-543`) gives the answer a second validation would.
Read from code and not traced per transaction in the logs: the refused batch's transactions stay in the worker's pool (`crates/batch-builder/src/lib.rs:420-430`), which outlives the epoch's batch builder, and go out again once the sender and the refusing peer are in the same epoch, as they would have without the cache, so nothing here needs the issue reopened.
One point to act on: this expected refusal is logged at error level on the sender (`crates/consensus/worker/src/quorum_waiter.rs:94-99`), 742 lines in r20261113-1630, and a lower level or a counter would keep it out of operators' error logs.
