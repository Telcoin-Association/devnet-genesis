Bench 6 data for this issue: both forms of the author-side fix (D) in four runs, the voter-side fix (V), and what `main` still does.

### Author side

One run per form and disk; a 600 × 16 load blast, validator-05 killed inside it.
Always-on: (D)'s rule on every header; as parents name earlier picks of that rule, every header names its author's executed tip minus 8 outputs, the floor.
Adaptive (`quorum_exec_block_starved_rounds` 4): the author's own tip until an own header is cancelled, then the quorum rule inside a starved window.

Own headers certified over proposed, mixed phase:

| node disk | always-on | adaptive |
|---|---|---|
| 20,000 IOPS | [r20261113-1351](https://benchmark.telcoin.network/runs/r20261113-1351/): 99.94 %, lowest 99.7 % (validator-06) | [r20261113-2118](https://benchmark.telcoin.network/runs/r20261113-2118/): 99.64 %, lowest 99.0 % (validator-02) |
| 50,000 IOPS | [r20261113-1630](https://benchmark.telcoin.network/runs/r20261113-1630/): 88.26 %, lowest 44.5 % (validator-06) | [r20261114-0000](https://benchmark.telcoin.network/runs/r20261114-0000/): 94.82 %, lowest 84.1 % (validator-06) |

In bench 5 ([r20261107-1504](https://benchmark.telcoin.network/runs/r20261107-1504/), 20,000 IOPS) validator-04, -08 and -10 certified 2, 0 and 149 of 918 own header rounds before the kill; refused-behind votes: 47,614 over that run, 0 and 15 over the 20,000 IOPS mixed phases.
No node logged a fork, halt or unexpected execution result.

At 20,000 IOPS the disk's write limit gave every engine the same cost; the lead almost never reached the floor (0.01-0.02 % of headers).
The adaptive form named a block about 0.9 s old against 5.5 s.

At 50,000 IOPS each engine ran at its own cost and the spread outgrew the floor: in r20261113-1630 validator-06 ran about 18 outputs ahead of validator-01, so four or five voters lacked the block a fast author named.
Inside its starved windows the adaptive form named the same block (tip minus 7.94-7.97 outputs); its higher certification came with a 6.8 % lower round rate before the kill, and one run cannot separate it from which validators lagged.

Recomputed on the measured tips before the kill, rounds held fixed, a floor of 16 outputs gives fleet certification of 97.0 % (r20261113-1630) and 100.0 % (r20261114-0000), 32 gives 99.9 % and 100.0 %: arithmetic, not a run, for a block 16 or 32 outputs old.

So (D) with a floor of 8 holds while the disk binds and, in either form, does not hold once the spread exceeds the floor.
Open: the form and the floor's depth, with the senders' cost riding on it: the adaptive form's senders resent 3.5 and 1.9 times as many transactions (20,000 and 50,000 IOPS; #1807).
On `main` at 45dce8947 the proposer still names its own newest block (`crates/consensus/primary/src/proposer.rs:385`).

### Voter side

`vote_queue_fix`, on in all four runs, does the 1 s execution wait before taking the author's vote lock and refuses at once a request older than the newest it has seen from that author.
`lock_ms` p95 was 0 on every voter in every window of the four runs; bench 5's validator-01 waited 7.3-8.5 s for the lock.

Read from code on `main` at 45dce8947, after #1822, #1839, #1842, #1844 and #1846: `vote_request` still takes the per-author lock (`crates/consensus/primary/src/network/handler.rs:1029-1033`) and holds it through `vote_inner` (`:1158-1180`) and its 1 s execution wait (`:1462-1507`).
Nothing answers a superseded request before it gets the lock (`:1138-1147`, `:1189-1195`).
The unit test `vote_queued_requests_of_one_author_wait_in_turn`, on `main` as it is, answers six queued requests of one author one second apart, the sixth after the 5 s vote timeout.

New since #1842 (read from code): `vote_inner` also waits under the lock, up to the vote timeout, for an attached parent parked as pending (`:1845-1855`, `:1552`); bench 6's fix does not move that wait.
