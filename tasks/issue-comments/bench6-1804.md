Bench 6 data for this issue: (D) in two forms over four runs, (V), and what `main` still does.

### Author side

One run per form and disk.
Always-on: (D)'s rule on every header; as parents name earlier picks of that rule, an author ahead of the quorum settles on its executed tip minus 8 outputs, the floor (in [r20261113-1351](https://benchmark.telcoin.network/runs/r20261113-1351/) nine of ten authors named exactly that; 7.59 outputs behind the tip on the mean).
Adaptive (`quorum_exec_block_starved_rounds` 4): the author's own tip until an own header is cancelled uncertified, then the quorum rule for a window of 4 rounds that doubles while headers keep being cancelled (up to 128).

Own headers certified over proposed, the 600 × 16 blast's mixed phase (validator-05's outage included):

| node disk | always-on | adaptive |
|---|---|---|
| 20,000 IOPS | [r20261113-1351](https://benchmark.telcoin.network/runs/r20261113-1351/): 99.94 %, lowest 99.7 % (validator-06) | [r20261113-2118](https://benchmark.telcoin.network/runs/r20261113-2118/): 99.64 %, lowest 99.0 % (validator-02) |
| 50,000 IOPS | [r20261113-1630](https://benchmark.telcoin.network/runs/r20261113-1630/): 88.26 %, lowest 44.5 % (validator-06) | [r20261114-0000](https://benchmark.telcoin.network/runs/r20261114-0000/): 94.82 %, lowest 84.1 % (validator-06) |

In bench 5 ([r20261107-1504](https://benchmark.telcoin.network/runs/r20261107-1504/), 20,000 IOPS) validator-04, -08 and -10 certified 2, 0 and 149 of 918 own header rounds before the kill; refused-behind votes: 47,661 over that run (the counters above), 0 and 15 over the 600 × 16 blast's mixed phase in r20261113-1351 and r20261113-2118.

At 20,000 IOPS the adaptive form named a block about 0.9 s old against 5.5 s on the mean; in the blast's first 120 s it certified 97.98 % against 100.0 % (lowest 96.3 %, validator-02).

At 50,000 IOPS the spread outgrew the floor: in r20261113-1630 validator-06 ran about 18 outputs ahead of validator-01 and 45 ahead of validator-05 (outputs inferred from rounds).
Inside its starved windows the adaptive form named the same block (tip minus 7.94-7.97 outputs); one run cannot separate its higher certification from which validators lagged.

So (D) with a floor of 8 holds in either form while the disk binds, in neither once the spread exceeds it.
Open: the form and the floor's depth; the senders' cost rides on the choice (the adaptive form's senders resent 3.5 and 1.9 times as many transactions at 20,000 and 50,000 IOPS; #1807).
On `main` at 45dce8947 the proposer still names its own newest block (`crates/consensus/primary/src/proposer.rs:385`).

### Voter side

`vote_queue_fix` (on in all four runs) waits for execution, up to 1 s, before taking the author's vote lock, and refuses at once a request older than one it already received from that author.
`lock_ms` p95 was 0 on every voter in every window of the four runs; bench 5's validator-01 waited 7.3-8.5 s for the lock on validator-04's requests at 17:45.
The author-side rule was on in all four runs too, so they show the level, not what the switch alone did; the slow voters still waited the full second, now before the lock (`prelock_wait_ms` p95 1,001 ms in r20261113-1630 and r20261114-0000).

Read from code on `main` at 45dce8947, after #1822, #1839, #1842, #1844 and #1846: `vote_request` still takes the per-author lock (`crates/consensus/primary/src/network/handler.rs:1029-1033`) and holds it through `vote_inner` (`:1158-1180`) and its 1 s execution wait (`:1462-1507`).
Nothing answers a superseded request before it gets the lock (`:1138-1147`, `:1189-1195`).
A requester's cancel does not reach the voter: a queued request, including one for a header the author has already cancelled, stays on the lock until it is answered or the transport's 10 s timeout drops it (`crates/network-libp2p/src/consensus.rs:2357-2409`, `crates/consensus/primary/src/network/mod.rs:3792-3812`), although the comment #1842 added at `handler.rs:1149-1153` says the network layer drops it.
New since #1842: `vote_inner` also waits under the lock, up to the vote timeout, for an attached parent parked as pending (`:1845-1855`, `:1552`); bench 6's fix does not move that wait.
A unit test run against `main` as it is, `vote_queued_requests_of_one_author_wait_in_turn` (it comes with the voter-side PR), answers six queued requests of one author one second apart, the sixth after the 5 s vote timeout.
