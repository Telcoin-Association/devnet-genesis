Bench 8 data for this issue: the inferred step, that a far certificate reaches the others after they built their next-round header and so goes unnamed, is now measured.

The node is a benchmark build, b1104905f (main at 45dce8947 plus what #1862 has since merged into main, from before its switches were removed, all switches on), with log lines the measurement needs that are not on main: `parents` on `header proposed`, and `certificate accepted` with `parents_sent`, `late_ms` and `round_certs`.
Ten validators in ten regions; validator-02 and validator-06 are the only two in Europe.
Two windows, one run each, not published:

- r20261116-2011, second light blast, 21:39:00-21:45:13 UTC;
- r20261117-1401, load blast at reduced load (about 5,360 tx/s landed), 15:25:54-16:05:54 UTC.

## Measured

- In 96.3-98.0 % of (near receiver, far certificate) pairs, the round r certificate was accepted after the receiver logged its own round r+1 `header proposed`, a median 200-229 ms later (p90 372-401 ms), both times on the receiver's clock.
- In no pair where the certificate came after the receiver's next header did that header name it: 0 of 8,428 and 0 of 8,731 (validator-02, validator-06; r20261116-2011), 0 of 53,141 and 0 of 53,759 (r20261117-1401), with any of the nine others as receiver.
- 76.3-88.0 % of far certificates were named by none of the eight near validators' next headers, and 0.00-0.65 % by four or more of the nine others.
- 17.0-27.6 % of far certificates were committed; every one named by four or more was (5 of 5, 44 of 44, 12 of 12).
- Control, validator-04: named by all seven other near validators for 99.8-100 % of its certificates, late in 0 of 7,518 and 13 of 48,356 pairs, committed 99.94-100 %.

The two windows agree within a few points, so the step does not come from saturation.

A far validator's certificate forms a median 286-297 ms after its own header, against 134-182 ms for six near validators (01, 03, 04, 07, 08, 09), and reaches the near validators a median 262-284 ms (p95 436-474 ms) after they handed their own parent set for that round to the proposer.
That the eight others reach quorum before Europe's certificates arrive is our reading of the geography, not measured.

Validator-01's `leader schedule updated` lines list validator-02 and validator-06 as `bad` in every update (206 of 206 in r20261116-2011, 384 of 384 in r20261117-1401), with a median score of 0 against 54-60 for those six.
What the score counts in code was not read, so its link to the late certificates is unknown.

## Read from code

On main at 5533ff73a, as in the benchmark build:

- `crates/consensus/primary/src/aggregators/certificates.rs:92-98` sends a round's certificates to the proposer at quorum (`:39-41`), then each later one alone (weight not reset, `:94`).
- `crates/consensus/primary/src/proposer.rs:1288` advances the round as the next header is built; `:1353` takes the parent set.
- A later round r certificate reaches `process_parents` (`:812`), whose `Less` arm (`:882-891`) drops it with only a trace log, "ignoring older parents".
- Nothing else carries it into a later header: `last_parents` is replaced, extended only at round r, or cleared.

The build logs `header proposed` after the take, so a certificate accepted after that line missed that header.
