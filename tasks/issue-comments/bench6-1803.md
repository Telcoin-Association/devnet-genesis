Bench 6 data for this issue: option (A) ran as a switch with G = 150 ms; it fixed the far pair at light load at a larger round-time cost than modelled, and it is not on main.

### What ran

The grace ran in an arm walk on the bench-6 fleet (r20261112-1520: nine node configurations applied one after another by rolling restart; it has no public page).
The walk's switches are cumulative, so the grace arm also had the earlier arms' switches on, among them the adaptive vote fix and pipelined persist; it ran an idle mark, a light 8 × 16 blast and a short 600 × 16 blast.
Unlike (A), the next round's leader proposes without the grace.

### Idle and light load

- Validators 02 and 06 went from 1.8-2.8 % of the committed certificates to 10.0 % each, the fair share; their never-committed certificates fell from 79-84 % to 0.0-0.6 %.
- Their generators went from 10.2 and 8.8 tx/s at a seen p50 of 15.2 and 15.9 s to 50.9 and 49.4 tx/s at 2.53 and 2.55 s.
- The grace armed on 94-95 % of headers, ended complete on 96 % of those, and waited 85-102 ms (median).
  The round grew from 345 to 441-443 ms (+28 %), against the model's 364-404 ms, and the fleet's floor seen p50 from 1.51 to 1.77 s.
- Their leader share did not recover, contrary to item (E): 0 % at light load, under 1 % at idle.
  Validator-10's fell from 11-13 % to 0.9 % at idle and 0 % at light load (5.1 % in the loaded blast), unexplained.

### Under load the cost is not "about 0"

In the 600 × 16 blast the grace armed on 6,699 of 7,109 headers, expired on 965, and the median round was 461.6 ms against 371.3 ms in the arm before (+24 %), though the two blasts also differed in length and cache state.
Executed throughput held (4,084 against 4,093 tx/s), but for the same reasons its effect on throughput is not settled.

### The headline runs left it off

It stayed off for validator-10's lost leader rounds and the missing measurement under sustained load.
In [r20261113-1351](https://benchmark.telcoin.network/runs/r20261113-1351/) the far pair again held 2.74 % and 2.68 % of committed certificates at light load, and their generators landed 11 % and 12 % of the other eight's median rate at a seen p50 of 17.3 and 15.9 s, as in bench 5.

### On main at 45dce8947

Unchanged: the next round's leader proposes with zero delay (`crates/consensus/primary/src/proposer.rs:566-576`), parents from an older round are ignored (`:771-780`), and there is no grace.
The bench-6 switch (`parent_grace`, on `bench/bench6-2026-11`) is not being ported to main now.
