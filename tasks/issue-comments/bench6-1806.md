Bench 6 data for this issue: the closes of five runs, the tracker-drain arm, the false quorum error and the vote forms; none of these bench-6 switches is being ported to main now.

### The closes

All 52 closes of five runs certified with at least 7 of 10 signers, each on one record hash and one closing block: 25 in an arm walk on the bench-6 fleet (r20261112-1520: nine node configurations applied one after another by rolling restart; it has no public page) and 27 in the four headline runs, [r20261113-1351](https://benchmark.telcoin.network/runs/r20261113-1351/) and [r20261113-1630](https://benchmark.telcoin.network/runs/r20261113-1630/) (always-on vote form, 20,000 and 50,000 IOPS, 6 closes each), [r20261113-2118](https://benchmark.telcoin.network/runs/r20261113-2118/) and [r20261114-0000](https://benchmark.telcoin.network/runs/r20261114-0000/) (adaptive form, 20,000 and 50,000 IOPS, 6 and 9 closes).
Loaded commit gaps were 3.5-14.5 s in r20261113-1351 and 3.4-10.3 s in r20261113-1630, against bench 5's 22.5 and 10.9 s.

The thinnest margin was r20261113-1630's 18:22:19 close, inside validator-05's outage: exactly 7 validators voted, validator-01 and -02 were still draining 15-output backlogs, and no eighth vote came.
Members missed a record by being late with the closing output (validator-01 at every loaded close of r20261113-1630, validator-05 in r20261113-1351) or early: a vote reaching a collector without its own record yet was dropped, so a certificate can show 7 signers when 8 or 9 voted.

The `epoch_behind` demotion fired: validator-05 twice at r20261113-1630's 18:02:19 close (4.05 s in all), and validator-02 for 4 minutes, from 30 s after epoch 8 opened, in r20261114-0000.

### The tracker-drain arm

The arm walk ran (D) as a switch: with it on, every node reaped every task it had tracked at the loaded close (55 to 1,310 tasks per node), but no validator overflowed with it off in that run either, so the fix was not exercised.
With it off, the overflow came back at loaded closes of r20261113-1630: validator-05 at the two loaded closes before its kill and validator-01 at the outage close (272 lines on validator-01 in that minute).

### The false quorum error and the vote forms

`failed to reach quorum on epoch close` (error level) fired 15 times in the five runs, each on a node that already held the certified record; its own vote round gave up about 65 s later (`timeouts=25`) and fetched the same hash.
Filed separately as {{false-quorum-error-on-epoch-close}}.

Neither vote form reaches the record path (read from code), and with one run each, their closes differed by no more than one run's own spread: at 50,000 IOPS the outage close's first quorum came at 12.82 s always-on (r20261113-1630) and 9.87 s adaptive (r20261114-0000), inside the 4.47-12.82 s of the always-on run's loaded closes.
