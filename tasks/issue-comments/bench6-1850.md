A question about the new stop.
`validate_downloaded_record` checks the certificate against the downloaded record's own committee, which may be a subset holding at least two thirds of the previous record's `next_committee` (`crates/storage/src/epoch_records.rs:410-411`, `crates/types/src/primary/epoch.rs:60-90`, `:145-162`).
After an ejection that leaves 7 of 10, 5 signatures pass (`super_quorum`, `:109-111`; worked in the doc at `:99-102`), so a conflicting record certified by those 5 stops the node.
Should the stop require a super-quorum of the full committee, 7 of the 10 members the previous record names?
