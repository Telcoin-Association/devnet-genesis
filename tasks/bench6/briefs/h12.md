## Why
Run 1 of bench 6 walks eight "arms" on one fleet for about seven hours: for each arm the sequencer (`benchmark/launch/run-arms.sh`)
applies a set of node switches with the arm tool (`benchmark/tools/params-apply.sh`), then runs blasts by calling the blast
driver's `fund` and `run` by hand with a fresh `BENCH_RUN_INDEX` each, records idle intervals as marks, and closes the arm.
Afterwards `benchmark/tools/arms-summary.py` must turn the results tree into one row per arm and blast, and the other readers
(`tools/cert-rate.py`, `tools/stage-latency.py`, `tools/block-rate.py`, `tools/propose-latency.py`, `report/provenance.py`) must
cut their windows by arm. Each of these pieces was built and tested by a different unit against its OWN idea of what the others
write. Nobody has put them together. The sequencer's unit reported one mismatch already: blasts run by hand are recorded in
`manifest.blast_runs[]` and in each blast's archived directory, not in `manifest.blasts[]` (which only the light-load sequence
writes, with `kind` and `arm`), while the summary tool's tests built their fixture around `blasts[]`. If the pieces do not fit,
the place to find out is here, not after seven hours of fleet time.
