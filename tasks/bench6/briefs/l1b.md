2. **`bench_lssd_mount_script <inst> <datadir>`** as §2.4 item by item, with l-common's decision 2 (no `blkid`). The device is `/dev/disk/by-id/google-local-nvme-ssd-0` only, must resolve to `/dev/nvme[0-9]*n[0-9]*` and must not hold `/`.
