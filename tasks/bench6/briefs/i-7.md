# I-7 — what the pool switches do to an output's contents

You are investigation **I-7**. Read `tasks/bench6/briefs/i-common-r1.md` first. Candidates 5, 7 and 8 of `A7/candidates.md` are yours. Skills: `tn-domain-worker`, `tn-domain-execution`.

## The observations
From the arm `pool-local-r2` to `gr150` (blasts 8-14) four switches were on together: `sealed_tx_keep`, `digest_dedupe`, `recovery_threads=3`, `epoch_close_task_drain`. In those blasts, and not before or after:
1. Distinct accounts per executed transaction rose from 0.44-0.46 to 0.65-0.80 at full load (unit r1-A8, `A8/writes-model.md` §2.5 and `A8/outputs.csv.gz`), which costs about 1.22 times the copied database pages per transaction.
2. Part of each output's transactions did not execute: 19 % in `pool-local-r2`, 6 % in `pipe` (r1-A8 §1: the output's count against executed receipts).
3. The generators saw duplicate batch digests (53 / 14 / 17 / 24 in blasts 8 / 9 / 10 / 12; in 103 of 108 the first block carried no transactions and a later one, 3-192 s later, carried them; r1-A1 `A1/pool-fields.md`), while the node's own dedupe counters stayed 0 (r1-A4e `A4e/execute.md`).
4. The batch builder counted 210.9 M sealed-transaction skips in blast 8 (about 43,900 per second per validator) with a pending pool near 10,244 against 230 before (r1-A4e).
5. A warn template `quorum_waiter RPCError … batch <H> was already rejected in this epoch`, 1,305 lines in the run, 602 at blast 8's epoch close, also seen with `digest_dedupe` off (r1-A3 `A3/census.md`).
And the benefit: no gap resends, nothing unlanded after the tail scan (r1-A1).

2. For 1 and 2: is an output's higher account density a consequence of fewer consecutive transactions per sender in one output (a sender's next nonce held back while its previous one is sealed but not executed), and are the non-executed transactions duplicates, stale nonces, or something else? Show it on the run's data for one validator and one blast with the switches on and one with them off (the `output executed`, `batch built`, `batch sealed` lines and the counters in `P/prom-export/` such as `tn_batch_builder_sealed_txs_skipped_total`, `tn_reth_sealed_txs_*`, `tn_engine_cross_producer_repacked_txs_total`, `tn_reth_invalid_txs_skipped_total`, `tn_batch_builder_peer_deferred_txs_total`).
3. What would keep the benefit (no gap resends) without the cost in 1 and 2? Options with the code location each would touch, no patch.
4. Is anything here a correctness risk (a transaction executed twice, dropped silently, or a batch accepted by some validators and rejected by others), or only waste? Say what the code guarantees and where.
