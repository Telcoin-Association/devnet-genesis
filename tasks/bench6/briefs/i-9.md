# I-9 — why the observer alone wrote whole blocks

You are investigation **I-9**. Read `tasks/bench6/briefs/i-common-r1.md` first. Candidate 6 of `A7/candidates.md` is yours. Skill: `tn-domain-storage`.

## The observation
In run 1's opening control (every switch off, a fresh fleet) the ten validators wrote their database in single pages (device bytes 1.13-1.20 times the page bytes, writes of about 5 KiB, no large folios). The observer, on the same image, machine and disk, wrote 154-230 KiB blocks at 6.5-10.9 times the page bytes with 98-99.9 % of its resident database pages in large folios: bench 5's write amplification, on one node. Its library prefault writes covered only 15-24 % of what a validator prefaulted. After the next arm's eviction, restart and advice it fell to 1.06 times (unit r1-A4p, `A4p/persist.md` §2 and `A4p/folios.json`).
Bench 5 saw the amplification on every node; local probes before bench 6 tied it to large page-cache folios and to the library's prefault being switched off when a second handle opens the environment without `NORDAHEAD` (`tasks/bench6-agent-rules.md`, the MDBX rule, and the bench-5 work it cites: `benchmark/private/bench5-r20261107-1504/I-B2/` summary files only).
